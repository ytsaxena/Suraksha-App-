 Privacy Policy – Suraksha App
Last updated:1/3/2026
Suraksha App (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a personal safety application designed to help users send emergency alerts to their selected contacts. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum information required for emergency and safety features:
a) Personal Information
SOS Contacts: Name and phone number of contacts selected by the user for emergency alerts.
b) Location Information
Approximate or precise location, only when the user triggers an SOS action.
c) Device Information
Device identifier, used only to identify the device for app functionality.
d) App Usage & Diagnostics
Basic app performance and crash data collected via Firebase to improve app stability.

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:
Sending SOS SMS alerts to user-selected contacts
Sharing the user’s location during emergency situations
Playing emergency alarm/siren sounds
Improving app performance and reliability
 We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. SMS & Emergency Communication
SMS messages are sent only when the user explicitly presses the SOS button
No messages are sent automatically or in the background
Message content is limited to emergency-related text only

4. Data Storage & Security
SOS contact data may be stored securely using Firebase services
We implement reasonable security measures to protect user data
We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with unauthorized third parties

5. Data Sharing
We do not share your personal information except:
When required by law
When necessary for core app functionality (e.g., Firebase services)

6. Permissions We Use
Suraksha App may request the following permissions:
Contacts – to allow users to select SOS contacts
SMS – to send emergency messages
Location – to share location during emergencies

All permissions are used only for safety and emergency features.

7. Children’s Privacy
Suraksha App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. User Control
Users can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
